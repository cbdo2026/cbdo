XXIII Brazilian Colloquium on Orbital Dynamics

The deadline for nominating candidates for the Wagner Sessin Awards is 31 July 2026. Candidates must be up to 35 years old.

The Awards are for young researchers who have demonstrated a strong dedication to scientific or technological research and potential for leadership and have made notable contributions in one of the two selected areas: Orbital Mechanics and Control and Dynamical and Planetary Astronomy.

The scientific-technological contributions are characterized by articles published in indexed scientific journals, full articles published in arbitrated proceedings with strict selection criteria, theses defended, and notable technological contributions resulting from activities carried out mainly in Brazil.

The Awards will be presented at the opening session of CBDO 2026.

Please find detailed information at http://www.astro.iag.usp.br/~dinamica/premiows.htm

2026 Wagner Sessin Award Recipients

Yeva Gevorgyan de Mendonça

Dynamical and Planetary Astronomy

Yeva Gevorgyan de Mendonça holds a PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of São Paulo (USP), with research in celestial mechanics and planetary geophysics. She is currently a postdoctoral researcher at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), working on numerical methods for nonlinear partial differential equations. Her research interests include planetary geophysics, rational mechanics, and numerical analysis.

Gabriel Antonio Caritá

Orbital Mechanics and Control

Gabriel Antonio Caritá holds a PhD in Space Engineering and Technology from the National Institute for Space Research (INPE) and is currently a postdoctoral researcher at UNESP in Rio Claro. His research focuses on celestial mechanics and orbital dynamics applied to astrodynamics and astronomy, including retrograde orbits, orbital transfers, and machine learning.
