XXIII Brazilian Colloquium on Orbital Dynamics

First Circular — 7 April 2026

The XXIII Brazilian Colloquium on Orbital Dynamics (CBDO) will be held from December 7 to 11, 2026, at Centro Universitário Geraldo Di Biase (UGB) in Volta Redonda, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

The event will bring together researchers, students, and professionals in the fields of Celestial Mechanics, Orbital Dynamics, Planetary Science, Space Dynamics and Control, and Nonlinear Dynamical Systems.

The Organizing Committee invites the community to complete the pre-registration form to assess interest in participating in the event. At this stage, there is no need to submit an abstract. Only those who complete the form will receive subsequent circulars.

Second Circular — 7 June 2026

We are pleased to announce that registration is now open for the XXIII Brazilian Colloquium on Orbital Dynamics, to be held in Volta Redonda, Rio de Janeiro, from December 7 to 11, 2026. This edition of the CBDO will be especially meaningful, as Professor Sylvio Ferraz Mello, one of the founders of the event, celebrates his 90th birthday this year while remaining fully active in research in dynamical astronomy.

Below, we provide some important information about the event. Additional information is available on the event website: access here, or by email at: cbdo2026@gmail.com.

1. Venue and schedule

The CBDO will be held at the Milton Carlos Auditorium, Centro Universitário Geraldo Di Biase - UGB, located at Rua Deputado Geraldo Di Biase, 81, Aterrado, Volta Redonda.

The event is expected to begin at 12:00 p.m. on December 7 and conclude at 12:00 p.m. on December 11.

2. Schedule

  • Deadline for registration and abstract submission: July 31, 2026.
  • Deadline for submitting applications for the Wagner Sessin Award: July 31, 2026.
  • Review of abstracts by the Scientific Committee: August 2026.
  • Notification of acceptance and publication of the final program: September 11, 2026.
  • Deadline for confirming participation: September 30, 2026.
  • Deadline for payment of the discounted registration fee, except for participants residing abroad: September 30, 2026.
  • Opening of the XXIII CBDO: December 7, 2026.

3. Mode of participation

The Scientific Committee has decided that participation will be exclusively in person. The event aims to value personal interaction among participants and to promote a productive environment for discussion. In order to maximize integration among the different areas covered by the event, all sessions will be plenary.

There are currently no plans for live streaming of the event. However, the possibility of recording the oral presentations and making them available afterwards is being considered.

Only in exceptional and duly justified cases may the possibility of a remote oral presentation be evaluated.

4. Registration and abstract submission

Registration and abstract submission are open until July 31, 2026, at 11:59 p.m., through the following form: access here.

Each participant may submit up to two (2) contributions as presenting author. The name of the presenting author must be indicated in bold in the abstract. There is no limit to participation in contributions as co-author.

The abstract must be written in English, must not exceed one (1) page, including any additional information such as references, acknowledgements, etc., and must not contain figures or tables.

The abstract must be prepared exclusively using the LaTeX template available at the following link: access here.

A compiled version of the abstract in PDF format must also be generated. At the time of registration, both the .tex file and the .pdf file must be attached. The files must be clearly identified with the name of the presenter, for example: Fulano_de_Tal_01.tex, Fulano_de_Tal_01.pdf, Fulano_de_Tal_02.tex, Fulano_de_Tal_02.pdf.

IMPORTANT: In accordance with the CNPq Policy on Integrity in Scientific Activity, the use of Artificial Intelligence at any stage of the development of the work(s) submitted to the CBDO must be explicitly declared in the corresponding abstract(s), indicating the name of the AI tool and the type of use, for example: language editing, preparation of slides, figures or tables, code generation, data analysis, etc.

Information about accommodation and transportation options is available on the event website: access here. The Organizing Committee is negotiating discounts with some hotels, and the corresponding information will be updated in due course.

7. Wagner Sessin Award

Members of the CBDO community who hold or have held leadership positions in research activities in Orbital Dynamics and related areas may submit nominations for the Wagner Sessin Award, in its two categories, until July 31, 2026. Researchers up to 35 years of age may be nominated. Nominations should be sent by email to: sylvio@iag.usp.br.

8. Event languages

The official languages of the XXIII CBDO are Portuguese, English, and “Portuñol”. However, due to the participation of foreign researchers, it is suggested that presentation slides and posters be preferably written in English.

9. Poster preparation

Posters must be prepared in A0 format and must have support rods at the top and bottom, with a string so that they can be hung on a tripod-type stand. It will not be possible to attach posters to walls or flat surfaces.

10. Code of Conduct

The organizers are committed to making the event productive, safe, and pleasant for everyone, regardless of nationality, gender, race, sexual orientation, religion, political opinions, disability, or physical appearance. Harassment or misconduct of any kind will not be tolerated, including physical, verbal, and online actions, among others.

Participants are requested to follow the guidelines below:

a) Behave professionally. Treat everyone with kindness and consideration. Be respectful and thoughtful when criticizing ideas, and be considerate of your fellow participants. Do not insult or demean other participants or staff members.

b) Harassment and sexist, racist, or exclusionary comments or jokes are not appropriate. Harassment includes, but is not limited to, sustained disruption of talks or other events, sexual comments, deliberate intimidation, stalking of any individual, and offensive language. It also includes offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, religion, or political opinions.

c) Communication is expected to be appropriate for a professional audience with diverse backgrounds. Sexual or sexist language is not appropriate.

Any violation of these guidelines may result in the immediate removal of the offender from the meeting without prior warning. In addition, the offender’s actions may be reported to the Organizing Committee. Retaliation against anyone who reports violations of this Code of Conduct will not be tolerated. The priority will be to restore a safe environment.
